# 🛒 Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis - Power BI Project

## 📌 Project Overview
An end-to-end Business Intelligence project analyzing retail superstore performance, financial profitability, growth trajectories (MoM and YoY), customer segmentation, and logistics fulfillment modes. The project features an interactive Power BI executive dashboard designed to track key retail drivers.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Metrics & Key Insights

### 1. Core Summary Metrics
* **Total Sales:** **$2.30M**
* **Total Profit:** **$286.40K**
* **Average Discount:** **0.16**
* **Quantity Sold:** **38K** units

### 2. Profit Trend & Growth Rates
* **Profit By Year:** A steady upward trend showing continuous business growth from **$50K in 2011** up to **$94K in 2014**.
* **MoM Growth Rate:** Monthly tracking bar chart analyzing month-over-month fluctuations across 2011 and 2012, highlighting peak spikes (such as April 2011 reaching 11).
* **YoY Growth Rate:** Year-over-year performance progression showing strong recovery and scaling in 2013 (**0.29**) and 2014 (**0.21**).

### 3. Customer Segmentation
* **Consumer Segment:** Dominates the market share with **1.16M (50.56%)** in total sales.
* **Corporate Segment:** Contributes **0.71M (30.74%)**.
* **Home Office Segment:** Accounts for **0.43M (18.7%)**.

### 4. Mode of Shipment (Logistics)
* **Standard Class:** Leads fulfillment by a significant margin with **96.33K (62.8%)**.
* **Second Class:** Accounts for **40.76K (26.57%)**.
* **First Class:** Comprises **13.84K (9.03%)**.
* **Same Day:** Smallest share at **2.45K (1.6%)**.
